## Statement of work

### Title

Sideout Analytics NCAA Women's Volleyball

### Abstract

This Statement of Work (SOW) outlines the objectives, scope,
deliverables, and timelines for the development and implementation of a new
NCAA Volleyball analytics system for Sideout Analytics. The project will provide a 
webapp that drives Sideout Analytics evaluation of NCAA women's volleyball. The webapp 
will provide features to visualize, query, and analyze game statistics at varying levels 
of granularity. In addition, an AI assistant will be added to assist in semantic-based 
ad-hoc analysis. The project will use public a public API, polling at a regular schedule. 
The timeline for completion is 2 months, with key milestones and deliverables
identified at each phase. The prototype project has no external budget, but is limited to the 
detailed list of features set in this document. An ongoing licensing cost structure and run and improve
 budget will be revisited after December's proof of concept.

### Value

The project serves as a lean prototype to show the system's core capabilities applied 
to Sideout Analytics business requirements. As such, the prototype will provide the basic 
value of automated data ingestion, processing, visualization, and analytics -- previously 
manual tasks handled by analysts. The tool will free their time for analytical application of 
their skillsets to drive business value. Any potential changes or additional requirements will 
be assessed and billed separately, subject to agreement by both parties.

### Scope

The scope of the project is limited to a proof of concept that Sideout Analytics 
can gain business value from a full, custom implementation. This prototype will therefore 
be limited to the core platform features of data ingestion, processing, visualization, and basic 
analytics and win prediction. Implementation and/or visualization of custom data or analytical models is out of scope. 

### Payment

No payment is due for the initial proof of concept delivery. A payment schedule for licensing and 
a custom implementation will be agreed on in the case that a full implementation is negotiated.

### People

Ethan -- the project manager, architect, and developer guiding the prototype. All client communication will be handled by him.

### Timeline

- **Data Ingestion and Processing Pipeline:** Demo on Nov 1
- **Ratings Model:** Demo Nov 15
- **Frontend and Predictions;** Demo Nov 22
- **Tournament Launch:** Demo Nov 29 (hardest deadline due to NCAA tournament selection to first play window)
- **AI Assistant:** Demo Dec 13
- **Evaluation of Predictor:** Report distributed by Dec 20

### Risks

1. This project hinges on receiving data from an open-source, unofficial API. If it goes down, then polling live data will not be available anymore
2. Timeline is tight for this prototype. The priority is to have the analytical backbone done by tournament selection. This is the bare minimum agreement. If it is not completed in time, the project fails. 
3. There is only one teammate working on the implementation. If a conflict arises, the project is at risk of failure.
